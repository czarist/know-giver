Know Giver — study for any exam (civil service, entrance exams, certifications), with AI and voice lessons

GETTING STARTED
1. Open Know Giver.
2. The first run asks for your language and region, your name, a teacher, the AI
   service with your key, and agreement to the terms. A short tour of the screens
   follows.
3. In Settings, download the voice models (speech recognition and the teacher's
   voice). They run on your computer.
4. In Exam notice, choose the notice PDF and generate your study plan.

UPDATES
When a new version is out, a bar at the top of the app offers it with a green
button. Settings > About and updates turns the check off.

YOUR DATA
Lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KnowGiver (database and downloaded models).
The AI key stays in the app's settings; never share a .env file.

UNINSTALL
Windows Settings > Apps > Know Giver, "Uninstall Know Giver" in the Start menu, or
unins000.exe in this folder. The uninstaller removes this folder and asks whether to
delete your study data too; if you say no, it stays.
